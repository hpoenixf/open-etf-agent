# Chart patterns from November 2026 earnings materials

These are reference patterns, not copied graphics or live chart inputs.

- [Tencent Q3 2026 results](https://www.tencent.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/E700_3Qresults.pdf): compare revenue and operating profit across reporting periods without mixing currencies or GAAP and non-GAAP bases. For a heatmap, pick comparable rates and one period definition; do not mix percentage points, percentages and RMB in the same color scale.
- [NVIDIA Q3 FY27 quarterly presentation](https://investor.nvidia.com/files/doc_financials/2027/Q327/NVDA-F3Q27-Quarterly-Presentation.pdf): the Data Center and Gross Margin slides distinguish period and accounting basis; the capital-return slide shows a dividend and buyback stack. Adapt its clear labeling and stable series identity, not its branding.
- [Alibaba September Quarter 2026 earnings presentation](https://data.alibabagroup.com/ecms-files/1567773279/2c7cfa39-3aa7-466f-b4d2-fb597bc48fd5/September%20Quarter%202026%20Results.pdf): the E-commerce composition and AI Cloud segment pages distinguish mix, revenue, growth and margin. Compare like-for-like lines or show a same-unit actual-versus-plan combination. Avoid implying that unlike units share an axis. Use area fills only when the zero baseline has analytical meaning, such as a signed flow.

None of these references is evidence that these companies used all four shapes. An adaptation must keep the period, units, definitions, and upstream evidence with the values. Never fill in unavailable values.
