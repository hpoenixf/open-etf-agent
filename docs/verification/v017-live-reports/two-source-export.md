## Side-by-side comparison

| Condition | Atlas A (A-2026-01) | Atlas B (B-2026-02) |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Observations

1. **Format coverage differs.** Atlas B's free edition supports two formats (CSV and Markdown), while Atlas A's free edition supports CSV only. Markdown export is therefore the one format present in B and absent in A.
2. **Row limit differs.** The per-export row cap rises from 120 in Atlas A to 300 in Atlas B — a difference of 180 rows per export.
3. **Online requirement is identical.** In both guides, every export requires an online connection, and the Enterprise edition adds PDF export without removing that online-export requirement.
4. **Consistent Enterprise behavior.** Neither version's Enterprise edition relaxes the connectivity condition; the only stated Enterprise benefit is the addition of PDF export.

## Summary

Moving from Atlas A to Atlas B broadens free-edition export formats (adding Markdown) and raises the per-export row cap from 120 to 300, while the online-export requirement and the Enterprise PDF addition remain unchanged.
