# Atlas Notes — Export and Offline-Use Conditions (Synthetic)

**Source:** Atlas Notes synthetic guide, version A-2026-01 (atlas-a), pages 1–2.
All statements below are drawn only from that fictional guide; no real product or financial data is involved.

## Export conditions

- **Free edition:** exports are **CSV only**, limited to **at most 120 rows per export**.
- **All exports require an online connection.**
- **Enterprise edition** adds **PDF export**, but it **does not remove the online-export requirement** — exports remain online-only in every edition described.

## Offline-use conditions

- **Free edition:** previously downloaded notes can be **viewed offline**, but they **cannot be edited offline**.
- **Enterprise edition:** **offline editing is permitted**.
- **Initial download and all synchronization require an online connection** in both editions.
