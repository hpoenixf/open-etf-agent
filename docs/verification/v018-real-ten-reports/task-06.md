# Atlas Notes — Export Conditions

**Source:** Atlas Notes synthetic guide, version A-2026-01, physical page 1 (`atlas-a@A-2026-01:p1`).

## Conditions

- **Free edition:** Exports are **CSV only**, limited to **at most 120 rows per export**.
- **Online connection:** **All exports require an online connection**, regardless of edition.
- **Enterprise edition:** Adds **PDF export**. It does **not** remove the online-export requirement.

## Summary

Export capability is CSV-only with a 120-row cap in the Free edition; the Enterprise edition adds PDF. In every case, an online connection is required to export.
