# Atlas Notes — Export and Offline-Use Conditions

**Source guide:** *Atlas Notes* synthetic guide, version A-2026-01. These conditions apply only to this synthetic guide.

## Export conditions

- The **free edition** exports **CSV only**, limited to **at most 120 rows per export**.
- The **enterprise edition** adds **PDF export**, but it does not remove the online-export requirement.
- **All exports require an online connection**, including enterprise-edition exports.

## Offline-use conditions

- The **free edition** can view previously downloaded notes offline, but **cannot edit them offline**.
- The **enterprise edition** permits **offline editing**.
- **Initial download and all synchronization require an online connection.**
