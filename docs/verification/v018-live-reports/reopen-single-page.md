# Atlas Notes — Export Conditions

**Source:** Atlas Notes synthetic guide, version B-2026-02, physical page 1 (`atlas-b@B-2026-02:p1`).

## Export formats

- **Free edition:** CSV and Markdown.
- **Enterprise edition:** Adds PDF export in addition to the free-edition formats.

## Limits and requirements

- Each export is limited to **at most 300 rows**.
- **All exports require an online connection.**
- The Enterprise edition's additional PDF export **does not remove the online-export requirement**.

## Summary

Export capability differs by edition only in the formats offered: free supports CSV and Markdown, while Enterprise adds PDF. The 300-row maximum and the online-connection requirement apply to exports regardless of edition.
