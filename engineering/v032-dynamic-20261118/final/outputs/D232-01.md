# Assets That Meet Investor Cash-Out Requests

**Source:** `redemptions.txt` (page 1) — Liquidity policy, current 2026.

## Supported policy

According to the liquidity policy:

1. **Cash and overnight deposits** cover investor withdrawals [1].
2. **If redemptions exceed the cash buffer**, the fund sells **short-dated government bills** [1].
3. **Borrowing and forced sales of illiquid bonds are prohibited** [1].

So the assets identified to meet investor cash-out (withdrawal/redemption) requests are cash and overnight deposits first, followed by short-dated government bills when redemptions exceed the cash buffer. Borrowing and forced sales of illiquid bonds are excluded by the stated policy [1].
