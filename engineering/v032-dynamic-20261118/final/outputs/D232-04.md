## Do Saturdays count toward the settlement delay?

**No — Saturdays do not count toward the settlement delay.** [1]

Per the settlement terms in the returned source [1]:

- Orders are submitted during exchange hours; ownership and cash transfer "on the next business day after the trade, known as T+1 settlement." [1]
- "Weekends and exchange holidays are not business days." [1]

Because Saturday falls on a weekend, and weekends are not business days, a Saturday cannot be one of the business days counted in the T+1 delay; the transfer date moves to the next business day. [1]

### Uncertainty

The text does not address any settlement period other than the T+1 next-business-day rule, so whether Saturdays count toward a different (non-T+1) delay is not stated in the returned evidence. [1]
