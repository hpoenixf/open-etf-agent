# Do Saturdays count toward the settlement delay?

**Answer: No.** Saturdays do not count toward the settlement delay.

## Evidence

The settlement document states that ownership and cash are transferred "on the next business day after the trade, known as T+1 settlement," and that "Weekends and exchange holidays are not business days." [1]

Because Saturdays fall within "weekends," which the source expressly excludes from business days, a Saturday is not counted in the T+1 business-day settlement delay; settlement occurs on the next *business* day after the trade. [1]

## Scope and limits

- The source speaks in terms of "weekends" and "business days" rather than naming Saturday individually; Saturday falls under "weekends." [1]
- The source does not state a separate rule for any particular exchange's holiday calendar beyond "exchange holidays"; no additional calendars are provided. [1]
- No document was found that treats Saturdays as business days or counts them toward settlement.

## Source

[1] settlement.txt, page 1 — "Trading and settlement. Investors submit orders during exchange hours. Ownership and cash are transferred on the next business day after the trade, known as T+1 settlement. Weekends and exchange holidays are not business days."
