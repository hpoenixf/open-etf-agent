This portable implementation was extracted and simplified from the author's ETF Agent engineering workflow. It includes no private operational evidence, credentials, financial data or application runtime.

Related projects and design influences:

- [OpenSpec](https://github.com/Fission-AI/OpenSpec), maintained by Fission AI, provides the optional schema format and spec-driven workflow ecosystem. OpenSpec has its own MIT license.
- [JiangWay's superpowers-bridge](https://github.com/JiangWay/openspec-schemas/tree/main/superpowers-bridge) and [Superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers) informed the original workflow's planning and TDD practices.

The evidence-driven schema shipped here is newly written, not a redistributed copy of superpowers-bridge. This kit does not claim authorship of OpenSpec, the general Spec/TDD workflow or those upstream projects. Installing OpenSpec is optional and independent of this package.
