# Hotspot interpretation

Use when a fresh event, release, market move or revised expectation needs interpretation. Do not rank items by headline drama.

1. Identify what changed versus the last confirmed state or comparable expectation. Verify the original event time and market session; distinguish a proposal, adopted rule, effective measure and actual result.
2. Trace the channel from the event to rates, orders, earnings, cash flows or risk appetite, then to the affected index or industry. Compare relevant breadth or groups only if informative. Keep observed contribution distinct from estimated attribution.
3. Test one plausible competing explanation. Say what remains unknown and name one dated release, filing or indicator that could discriminate between explanations. Do not use synchronized price movement alone as causal proof.

Use short-term context only as needed. This is a method, not a source or a mandatory answer format.
