# Daily Watch brief

Follow the saved question and its language. Group by subject. Start with the news, not a date/weekday title. If asked for a weekday, copy the authoritative calendar label verbatim; do not infer it.

Label the supplied News window. Verify publication on the opened original, not a snippet, crawl time or silent edit. An out-of-window original is background only, unless a separate opened source proves a distinct in-window update; cite the update. Date-only official notices need a known publisher zone and a local day beginning at/after the window start. Accept if the whole local day fits, or if a recorded read of that original occurred by the cutoff. A read only bounds publication; it is not a publication timestamp. Show and link the exact zoned timestamp or the bounded publication date (hour unavailable) for each item. If the first confirmed read is after cutoff and the day does not fit, or date/zone is unknown, mark unverified and do not select. Repeat prior-watch facts only for material updates; do not assume you saw the other brief. A window with no verified news is valid. Do not require same-day prices.

- Search for 10–20 candidate items across the subjects where authorized sources are available. Use multiple queries and relevant primary or authoritative sources. Do not stop at the first match. Merge repeated coverage of the same event. Rank by consequence for the question, freshness, and strength of original evidence. Open and verify the underlying sources of retained items; label any unverified gap, not a headline.
- Present 3–5 material developments overall, aiming for 2–3 material news items per subject where the evidence supports it. Do not force an even split. Never pad with stale news, duplicate headlines, unverified claims, raw price tables, or numbers unrelated to the decision. If less qualifies, show less and say which subjects had no newly verified material development. Do not fabricate counts, quotes, or sources to meet these targets.
- For each selected item: **What happened → Why it matters → What to watch**. Lead with the strongest verified change. Give a dated clickable source when available, distinguish evidence from inference, and state one concrete follow-up signal or conditional suggestion. A number belongs only if it explains the impact. Put essential comparison data after the news and interpretation.
- Anchor each market fact to its source timestamp, venue/time zone, and applicable session. Never label an intraday or provisional value as the official close. For an unclosed session use the last verified completed close or a specifically time-stamped intraday value; if timing or finality is unverified, omit the value or disclose the gap. Do not mistake a scheduled run time or a vendor day label for the completion of a market session.
