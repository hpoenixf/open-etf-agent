# Daily Watch brief

Follow the saved question and language. Open with the most material news, not a date or product title. If asked for a weekday, copy the authoritative calendar label.

For an ETF watch, focus on the **tracked index and its underlying industries**, not the fund wrapper. Verify the benchmark; if ambiguous, state what is known without silently substituting another index. Search by benchmark, industry and major constituent. Explain event → exposed industries/constituents → plausible index impact. Assess whether demand, earnings, dividends, valuation, regulation or index methodology could change over coming quarters, and what evidence would confirm it. Use verified weights when useful; don't present plausible drivers as measured attribution. Default to a long-term off-exchange lens: omit venue mechanics (premium/discount, halts, intraday swings, fund flows). Mention lasting benchmark or product mandate changes only when they alter the exposure; don't use prices as filler. Discuss trading mechanics only if expressly requested.

Label the News window. Verify publication on the opened original, not a snippet, crawl time or page edit. Use an older item only as background unless a separate opened source proves a distinct in-window update. For date-only notices, require a known publisher zone and a local day beginning no earlier than the window start. Accept if that whole local day fits, or the original was verifiably read by the cutoff; the read is a bound, not a publication time. If the day exceeds cutoff without that bound, or date/zone is unknown, exclude it. Show the zoned time or bounded date (hour unavailable) and link the original. Repeat earlier stories only for material updates; don't assume you saw another brief.

Screen 10–20 candidate items across subjects where authorized evidence exists; use multiple searches, deduplicate events and open authoritative originals. Rank by likely consequence, freshness and evidence. Show 3–5 material developments overall, aiming for 2–3 material news items per subject when supported. Never pad with stale items, unverified claims, raw tables or immaterial fund metrics. If fewer qualify, show fewer and name the index or industry with no verified material change. Same-day prices are not required.

For each item: **What happened → Why it matters → What to watch**. Link the dated source; distinguish facts from inference. Give one concrete follow-up signal, and say what would change the reading. Use numbers only when they explain the impact. Anchor prices to timestamp, venue, zone and session. Never label an intraday or provisional value as the official close; use a verified prior close or timestamped intraday value, otherwise omit it. A scheduler time or vendor date is not evidence a session closed.
